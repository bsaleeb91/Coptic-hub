---
name: analyze
description: Deep analysis of a problem, dataset, or situation — gathers context from all sources before answering
---
Invoke the researcher agent first to gather all relevant context.
Then analyze the situation thoroughly:
1. What is the current state?
2. What patterns or issues are visible?
3. What is the root cause if something is wrong?
4. What are the options or recommendations?
5. What needs human decision?

Invoke the reflector agent before presenting the final analysis.
Return findings clearly with ✅ ⚠️ 🔴 for severity tiering.
