Review the entire conversation and capture what was learned.

Steps:
1. Review everything discussed in this session
2. Identify what was genuinely new, corrected, or confirmed:
   - New rules or gotchas discovered
   - Mistakes corrected
   - Processes clarified
   - Data issues found and resolved
   - Ownership or contact clarifications
3. Propose entries in this format:
   (YYYY-MM-DD) [Domain] Lesson: what was learned or confirmed
4. Also check: does anything in reference files need updating?
   - DOMAIN.md, RULES.md, REPORTS.md, TEAM.md
5. Tell the user: "Copy the entries above into agent-skill/references/MEMORY.md to save them permanently."

Only propose entries that are genuinely useful.
Quality over quantity — 2 great entries beat 10 vague ones.
Agent never writes files directly — always proposes, user commits.
