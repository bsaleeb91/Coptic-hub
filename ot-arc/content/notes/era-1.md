## Talking points
- **The palace.** Creation is a royal palace built for a king and queen. From the first page, God's plan is to live *with* us.
- **"Where are you?"** God is not asking for information. He is the one walking in the garden, looking for the people who are hiding from Him. He doesn't wait for them to come back and apologize. He goes to them.
- **Coats of skin.** Something dies to cover their shame, and God does the covering. This is the first picture in the sacrifice storyline.
- **The pattern.** After every collapse, God narrows His focus instead of giving up: a promise after the Fall, a covenant after the flood, one man called after Babel.

## Questions for the class
- Why would God ask a question He already knows the answer to? What was He inviting Adam to do?
- Adam and Eve hid. What do people hide behind today when they know they have done wrong?
- After Babel, God starts over with one man, Abraham. Why start smaller instead of starting from scratch?

## The storylines here
- **Father:** A promise after the Fall, a covenant after the flood, a call after Babel. The cycle begins: God loves, man sins, God comes looking.
- **Teacher:** The coats of skin, Abel's offering, Isaac on the mountain. The first pictures of love that costs something.
- **Neighbor:** Eden is God's home with man. God walks in the garden, and man walks out.
