## Talking points
- **The cycle.** God loves, man sins, man repents, God forgives, on repeat through every era.
- **Punishment as education.** In Fr. Tadros Malaty's framing, the consequence is how a Father teaches, not an offended King getting paid. Consequences are real, but their purpose is to bring the child home.
- **Across the four eras.** A promise after the Fall. The judges cycle. The prophets' warnings. The exile, and then the return.

## Questions for the class
- What's the difference between a punishment meant to hurt and one meant to teach?
- In each era, where do you see God starting over instead of giving up?
- Why does God keep forgiving a people who keep falling back into the same sins?
