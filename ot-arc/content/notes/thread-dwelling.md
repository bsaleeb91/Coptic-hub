## Talking points
- **God moves in.** First the tabernacle, then the Temple, then the human heart.
- **Follow the arc.** God walks in the garden, then pitches a tent in the middle of the camp. Glory fills the Temple, then the glory departs. Then comes John 1:14.
- **The prophets were already pointing inward.** Haggai's aim is building the inner temple of the heart. Zechariah shows Christ building His temple. Ezekiel opens hope by building the Messianic temple. The temple of the heart is not a New Testament afterthought.

## Questions for the class
- Why could no building ever be enough for God?
- If God wants to live in the human heart, what does it look like to keep that house ready for Him?
- Moses built the house and couldn't finish it; the prophets built on it and couldn't finish it either. Who finishes it?
