## Talking points
- **The escalation.** The coats of skin, then Abel, then Isaac on the mountain, then the Passover lamb, then the Levitical system, then Christ.
- **Pictures, not a transaction.** God teaches through pictures what love that costs everything looks like. Each picture is clearer than the one before, until the picture becomes a Person.
- **Hebrews 9–10** makes this argument explicitly. It's the place to send anyone in the class who wants to read further.

## Questions for the class
- Why would God teach with pictures for so long before sending His Son?
- Isaac carried the wood up the mountain. Who does that remind you of, and why?
- Which picture in the list hits you hardest?
