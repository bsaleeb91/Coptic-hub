## Judgment calls to look at first

1. **John 14:28 ("The Father is greater").** The brief's explanation was
   "spoken as the incarnate Son." Athanasius's own reading in *Against the
   Arians* I.58 is different: the Father is greater as the source the Son is
   begotten from, not in nature. The card follows Athanasius. If you'd rather
   teach the "in His humanity" reading (Cyril and others), say so.
2. **Proverbs 8:22.** Athanasius reads "created me" as the Son's human body
   (II.47), so John 1:14 is the main counter, with John 1:3 also accepted. The
   verse text is the Greek wording Arius used, as Athanasius quotes it. The NKJV
   says "The LORD possessed me."
3. **Acts 2:36 unlocks "God from God, Light from Light."** The pairing works
   through the Hebrews 1:3 counter (the Son as the Father's radiance), but the
   link is looser than the others.
4. **On the Incarnation cards as answers.** Four of them are now correct
   answers: King in the City (to "God Made Him Lord"), Restored Portrait (to
   "Greater Than I"), God Became Man (to "Not Even the Son Knows"), and Death
   Trampled (to "He Came to Be From Nothing"). Check that each pairing makes
   sense to you. "Worship Belongs to God" is from *Against the Arians* and is
   still partial credit only.
5. **Colossians 1:15 in the NKJV** reads "firstborn *over* all creation," which
   already leans toward the Orthodox reading. Arius used the Greek "firstborn
   *of* all creation." This challenge may feel easier than intended.
6. **The §54 line** in this translation reads "assumed humanity that we might
   become God," not the more familiar "became man."

## The 325 Creed (Robertson, public domain)

> We believe in One God, the Father Almighty, Maker of all things visible and invisible: And in One Lord Jesus Christ, the Son of God, begotten of the Father, Only-begotten, that is, from the essence of the Father; God from God, Light from Light, Very God from Very God, begotten not made, One in essence with the Father, by Whom all things were made, both things in heaven and things in earth; Who for us men and for our salvation came down and was made flesh, was made man, suffered, and rose again the third day, ascended into heaven, and cometh to judge quick and dead. And in the Holy Ghost. And those who say, "Once He was not," and "Before His generation He was not," and "He came to be from nothing," or those who pretend that the Son of God is "Of other subsistence or essence," or "created" or "alterable," or "mutable," the Catholic Church anathematizes.

Source: Creed of Nicaea (325), as quoted by Eusebius of Caesarea, Letter to his Diocese §4. Trans. A. Robertson, Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers, 2nd Series, Vol. 4 (1892), public domain.

## Arius's challenges (8)

### [ ] The Lord Created Me  `ari-prov-8-22`

**Arius plays:** Proverbs 8:22 (Greek Septuagint): *"The Lord created me a beginning of His ways, for His works"*  
**Card text:** Arius: Wisdom says 'The Lord created me.' The Son is Wisdom. So the Son was created!  
**Correct counters:** The Word Became Flesh (John 1:14); All Things Through Him (John 1:3)  
**Unlocks Creed slot:** "begotten not made"

**Explanation popup:** Proverbs speaks in riddles, so Athanasius says to ask who is speaking, and when. 'Created' points to the human body the Son took on to save us, not to His Godhead. As God, He is the One through whom all things were made.

> "My Father has prepared for Me a body, and has created Me for men in behalf of their salvation."
> — Against the Arians II.47 (NPNF trans.)

**Citation:** St. Athanasius, Against the Arians II.44-47

*Note:* Verse text is as Athanasius quotes it in Against the Arians II.44. Arius relied on the Greek 'created'; the NKJV, from the Hebrew, reads 'The LORD possessed me.'

### [ ] Firstborn Over All Creation  `ari-col-1-15`

**Arius plays:** Colossians 1:15: *"the firstborn over all creation"*  
**Card text:** Arius: 'Firstborn over all creation.' First of the creatures is still a creature!  
**Correct counters:** By Him All Things (Colossians 1:16-17); The Only-Begotten (John 1:18)  
**Unlocks Creed slot:** "begotten of the Father, Only-begotten"

**Explanation popup:** 'Firstborn' doesn't mean the first thing made. The very next verse says all things were created by Him. He is the Only-begotten Son, and He is called 'Firstborn' because He became a Brother to us.

> "because of the Word's condescension to the creatures, according to which He has become the 'Brother' of 'many.'"
> — Against the Arians II.62 (NPNF trans.)

**Citation:** St. Athanasius, Against the Arians II.62-63

*Note:* Arius read the Greek 'firstborn of all creation.' The NKJV's 'over all creation' already leans toward the Orthodox reading, which may make this challenge feel easier.

### [ ] Greater Than I  `ari-john-14-28`

**Arius plays:** John 14:28: *"My Father is greater than I."*  
**Card text:** Arius: Jesus Himself said the Father is greater. So the Son is lesser!  
**Correct counters:** I and My Father Are One (John 10:30); Seen Me, Seen the Father (John 14:9); The Restored Portrait (argument)  
**Unlocks Creed slot:** "One in essence with the Father"

**Explanation popup:** Jesus said 'greater,' not 'better.' Athanasius explains that the Father is greater because the Son is begotten from Him, not because the Son is a lesser kind of being. The same Jesus said, 'I and my Father are one.'

> "'greater,' not indeed in greatness, nor in time, but because of His generation from the Father Himself"
> — Against the Arians I.58 (NPNF trans.)

**Citation:** St. Athanasius, Against the Arians I.58

*Note:* This follows Athanasius's own reading (the Father as source). Other Fathers also read this verse as spoken in Christ's humanity; the brief's original explanation used that reading.

### [ ] Not Even the Son Knows  `ari-mark-13-32`

**Arius plays:** Mark 13:32: *"But of that day and hour no one knows, not even the angels in heaven, nor the Son, but only the Father."*  
**Card text:** Arius: The Son doesn't know the day or hour. God knows everything. So the Son isn't God!  
**Correct counters:** Spoken as Man (argument); The Form of a Bondservant (Philippians 2:6-7); God Became Man (argument)  
**Unlocks Creed slot:** "for us men and for our salvation came down and was made flesh, was made man"

**Explanation popup:** The Son truly became man, and He speaks sometimes as God and sometimes as man. Not knowing the hour belongs to the human nature He took on for us, not to the Word who made the world.

> "He made this as those other declarations as man by reason of the flesh"
> — Against the Arians III.43 (NPNF trans.)

**Citation:** St. Athanasius, Against the Arians III.42-43

### [ ] The Only True God  `ari-john-17-3`

**Arius plays:** John 17:3: *"that they may know You, the only true God, and Jesus Christ whom You have sent."*  
**Card text:** Arius: Jesus called the Father 'the only true God.' That leaves the Son out!  
**Correct counters:** This Is the True God (1 John 5:20)  
**Unlocks Creed slot:** "Very God from Very God"

**Explanation popup:** 'Only true God' is said against false gods, not against the Son. Jesus even names Himself right next to the Father in the same sentence. And John calls the Son Himself 'the true God.'

> "this is said not to the denial of Him who said, 'I am the Truth,'"
> — Against the Arians III.9 (NPNF trans.)

**Citation:** St. Athanasius, Against the Arians III.9

### [ ] God Made Him Lord  `ari-acts-2-36`

**Arius plays:** Acts 2:36: *"God has made this Jesus, whom you crucified, both Lord and Christ."*  
**Card text:** Arius: 'God has MADE Him Lord.' If He was made Lord, He wasn't Lord before!  
**Correct counters:** Radiance of His Glory (Hebrews 1:3); The Form of a Bondservant (Philippians 2:6-7); The King in the City (argument)  
**Unlocks Creed slot:** "God from God, Light from Light"

**Explanation popup:** Read the whole verse: 'this Jesus, whom you crucified.' Peter is talking about the Son as man, shown to be Lord after the Cross, not about when the Son began. He is forever the radiance of the Father's glory.

> "provided they attend to the context"
> — Against the Arians II.12 (NPNF trans.)

**Citation:** St. Athanasius, Against the Arians II.12

### [ ] Once He Was Not  `ari-once-not`

**Arius plays:** Slogan: *"Once He was not."*  
**Card text:** Arius: A father exists before his son. So there was a time when the Son did not exist!  
**Correct counters:** In the Beginning (John 1:1); Light and Its Shine (argument)  
**Unlocks Creed slot:** "from the essence of the Father"

**Explanation popup:** If the Son once didn't exist, then God was once without His own Word and Wisdom, like a light with no shine. But God has always been Father, so He has always had His Son.

> "'God was not always a Father.'"
> — Against the Arians I.5 (NPNF trans.)

**Citation:** Creed of Nicaea (325), anathemas; St. Athanasius, Against the Arians I.5, I.14

*Note:* The slogan's wording is from the anathemas of the 325 Creed. The quote shows Athanasius reporting Arius's own words from the Thalia.

### [ ] He Came to Be From Nothing  `ari-from-nothing`

**Arius plays:** Slogan: *"He came to be from nothing."*  
**Card text:** Arius: God made everything from nothing, and that includes the Son.  
**Correct counters:** Only the Maker Can Remake (argument); All Things Through Him (John 1:3); Death Trampled (argument)  
**Unlocks Creed slot:** "by Whom all things were made"

**Explanation popup:** Anything made from nothing is a creature, and a creature can't rescue creation. Only the Word who made all things out of nothing could make us new again.

> "Who, save the Word of God Himself, Who also in the beginning had made all things out of nothing?"
> — On the Incarnation §7 (C.S.M.V. trans.)

**Citation:** Creed of Nicaea (325), anathemas; St. Athanasius, On the Incarnation §7

## Creed slots (8)

- [ ] **1. "begotten of the Father, Only-begotten"**: Against calling the Son the first and greatest creature. He is the one and only Son. *(unlocked by beating Firstborn Over All Creation)*
- [ ] **2. "from the essence of the Father"**: The Son comes from the Father's own being, not from nothing, so He shares the Father's eternal life. *(unlocked by beating Once He Was Not)*
- [ ] **3. "God from God, Light from Light"**: Like light and its shine, the Son comes from the Father and was never apart from Him. *(unlocked by beating God Made Him Lord)*
- [ ] **4. "Very God from Very God"**: Against calling the Son 'god' only as an honorary title. ('Very' is old English for 'true.') *(unlocked by beating The Only True God)*
- [ ] **5. "begotten not made"**: Against calling the Son a creature. A father begets a son; a sculptor makes a statue. *(unlocked by beating The Lord Created Me)*
- [ ] **6. "One in essence with the Father"**: The key word, homoousios: the Son is the same God as the Father, not a lesser being. *(unlocked by beating Greater Than I)*
- [ ] **7. "by Whom all things were made"**: The Son is the Maker, not part of what was made. *(unlocked by beating He Came to Be From Nothing)*
- [ ] **8. "for us men and for our salvation came down and was made flesh, was made man"**: God Himself became man to save us. His humble human words don't make Him less than God. *(unlocked by beating Not Even the Son Knows)*

*End of match note:* In 381 the Council of Constantinople expanded this Creed, adding more about the Holy Spirit. That longer version is the Creed you pray in the Liturgy.

## Scripture cards (13)

### [ ] In the Beginning  `scr-john-1-1`

**John 1:1:** *"In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God."*  
**Card text:** Before anything began, the Word already WAS.  
**Cost 3 · Power 4** · Correct answer to: Once He Was Not

**Explanation:** John doesn't say the Word came to be in the beginning. He says the Word already was. When time began, the Son was already there, and He was God.

> "He ever was and is and never was not."
> — Against the Arians I.9 (NPNF trans.)

**Citation:** John 1:1; St. Athanasius, Against the Arians I.9

### [ ] All Things Through Him  `scr-john-1-3`

**John 1:3:** *"All things were made through Him, and without Him nothing was made that was made."*  
**Card text:** Everything that was made, He made. So He isn't one of the made things.  
**Cost 3 · Power 4** · Correct answer to: The Lord Created Me, He Came to Be From Nothing

**Explanation:** If not one thing was made without the Word, then the Word can't be one of the things that were made. He stands on the Maker's side of the line, not the creature's side.

> "the renewal of creation has been wrought by the Self-same Word Who made it in the beginning"
> — On the Incarnation §1 (C.S.M.V. trans.)

**Citation:** John 1:3; St. Athanasius, On the Incarnation §1

### [ ] The Word Became Flesh  `scr-john-1-14`

**John 1:14:** *"And the Word became flesh and dwelt among us"*  
**Card text:** God the Word took on a real human body.  
**Cost 2 · Power 3** · Correct answer to: The Lord Created Me

**Explanation:** The Word didn't stop being God when He became man. He put on our flesh. So when Scripture talks about the Son being 'made' or 'created,' look for His human body, not His Godhead.

> "we do not conceive the whole Word Himself to be flesh, but to have put on flesh and become man"
> — Against the Arians II.47 (NPNF trans.)

**Citation:** John 1:14; St. Athanasius, Against the Arians II.47

### [ ] The Only-Begotten  `scr-john-1-18`

**John 1:18:** *"The only begotten Son, who is in the bosom of the Father, He has declared Him."*  
**Card text:** Not the first of many. The ONLY Son.  
**Cost 3 · Power 4** · Correct answer to: Firstborn Over All Creation

**Explanation:** 'Only-begotten' means there is no one else like Him. He isn't the first in a line of creatures. He is the one and only Son, who lives in the Father's heart.

> "the term 'Only-begotten' is used where there are no brethren, but 'First-born' because of brethren"
> — Against the Arians II.62 (NPNF trans.)

**Citation:** John 1:18; St. Athanasius, Against the Arians II.62

### [ ] I and My Father Are One  `scr-john-10-30`

**John 10:30:** *"I and My Father are one."*  
**Card text:** The Son says it Himself: one with the Father.  
**Cost 4 · Power 5** · Correct answer to: Greater Than I

**Explanation:** Jesus doesn't say 'I agree with my Father.' He says 'we are one.' The Son shares the Father's own nature: one God, not a lesser god next to the real one.

> "proper to the Father's essence and one in nature with it"
> — Against the Arians I.58 (NPNF trans.)

**Citation:** John 10:30; St. Athanasius, Against the Arians I.58

### [ ] Seen Me, Seen the Father  `scr-john-14-9`

**John 14:9:** *"He who has seen Me has seen the Father"*  
**Card text:** Look at the Son and you see the Father.  
**Cost 3 · Power 4** · Correct answer to: Greater Than I

**Explanation:** No creature could say this. Only someone who is the perfect image of the Father, sharing everything the Father is, can show us the Father just by being seen.

> "He is the expression of the Father's Person, and Light from Light"
> — Against the Arians I.9 (NPNF trans.)

**Citation:** John 14:9; St. Athanasius, Against the Arians I.9

### [ ] The Form of a Bondservant  `scr-phil-2-6-7`

**Philippians 2:6-7:** *"who, being in the form of God, did not consider it robbery to be equal with God, but made Himself of no reputation, taking the form of a bondservant"*  
**Card text:** Equal with God, He chose to become a servant for us.  
**Cost 4 · Power 5** · Correct answer to: Not Even the Son Knows, God Made Him Lord

**Explanation:** Paul says the Son was already in the form of God, equal with God. Then He humbled Himself and became a servant. He wasn't promoted to God; He was God who stepped down for us.

> "existing as God, He took the form of a servant, and in taking it, was not promoted but humbled Himself"
> — Against the Arians I.40 (NPNF trans.)

**Citation:** Philippians 2:6-7; St. Athanasius, Against the Arians I.40

### [ ] By Him All Things  `scr-col-1-16-17`

**Colossians 1:16-17:** *"For by Him all things were created that are in heaven and that are on earth... And He is before all things, and in Him all things consist."*  
**Card text:** Heaven, earth, angels: all made by Him. He comes before all.  
**Cost 3 · Power 4** · Correct answer to: Firstborn Over All Creation

**Explanation:** Right after calling Him 'firstborn,' Paul says all things were created by Him and He is before all things. So 'firstborn' can't mean 'first creature.' It means He is first in rank over all creation.

> "because in Him the creation came to be"
> — Against the Arians II.63 (NPNF trans.)

**Citation:** Colossians 1:16-17; St. Athanasius, Against the Arians II.63

### [ ] Radiance of His Glory  `scr-heb-1-3`

**Card text:** The Son is the shining of the Father's glory.  
**Cost 3 · Power 4** · Correct answer to: God Made Him Lord

**Explanation:** Light and its shine always exist together. You never have a sun that hasn't started shining yet. The Son is the Father's radiance, so He has always been with the Father.


**Citation:** Hebrews 1:3

> "This is the True God and eternal life"
> — Against the Arians III.9 (NPNF trans.)

**Citation:** 1 John 5:20; St. Athanasius, Against the Arians III.9

## Athanasius argument cards (8)

### [ ] Only the Maker Can Remake  `arg-creator`

**Card text:** Sin broke creation. Only its Maker can fix it.  
**Cost 4 · Power 5** · Correct answer to: He Came to Be From Nothing

**Explanation:** Athanasius asks who could save us from death and decay. A creature can't re-create other creatures. Only the One who made everything from nothing can make us new, so our Savior must be God.

> "Who, save the Word of God Himself, Who also in the beginning had made all things out of nothing?"
> — On the Incarnation §7 (C.S.M.V. trans.)

**Citation:** St. Athanasius, On the Incarnation §7

### [ ] The Restored Portrait  `arg-portrait`

**Card text:** A ruined portrait needs the original to sit again.  
**Cost 3 · Power 4** · Correct answer to: Greater Than I

**Explanation:** When a portrait is ruined, the artist doesn't throw out the canvas; the person in it sits again. We were made in God's image, so the Father's true Image came Himself to restore us. Only One who shares the Father's nature could be that Image.

> "He, the Image of the Father, came and dwelt in our midst"
> — On the Incarnation §14 (C.S.M.V. trans.)

**Citation:** St. Athanasius, On the Incarnation §14

### [ ] The King in the City  `arg-king`

**Card text:** One King moves into one house. The whole city is safe.  
**Cost 3 · Power 4** · Correct answer to: God Made Him Lord

**Explanation:** When a great king lives in one house, robbers stay away from the whole city. The King of all didn't become King by moving in. He was King already, and took one human body to defeat the enemy for all of us.

> "because of his dwelling in that single house, the whole city is honoured"
> — On the Incarnation §9 (C.S.M.V. trans.)

**Citation:** St. Athanasius, On the Incarnation §9

### [ ] Death Trampled  `arg-death-mocked`

**Card text:** Even kids laugh at death now. It's been beaten.  
**Cost 3 · Power 4** · Correct answer to: He Came to Be From Nothing

**Explanation:** If you saw boys teasing a lion, you'd know the lion was dead or powerless. Christian martyrs, even young ones, faced death without fear. That shows Christ really defeated it, which no creature could do.

> "nor, if he sees boys making fun of a lion, does he doubt that the brute is either dead or completely bereft of strength"
> — On the Incarnation §29 (C.S.M.V. trans.)

**Citation:** St. Athanasius, On the Incarnation §29

### [ ] God Became Man  `arg-theosis`

**Card text:** He became what we are so we could share in what He is.  
**Cost 4 · Power 4** · Correct answer to: Not Even the Son Knows

**Explanation:** The Son took on our humanity so that we could share in God's own life. So when He speaks humbly as a man, He is still God, who became man for us. A creature couldn't give us God's life.

> "He, indeed, assumed humanity that we might become God."
> — On the Incarnation §54 (C.S.M.V. trans.)

**Citation:** St. Athanasius, On the Incarnation §54

### [ ] Light and Its Shine  `arg-radiance`

**Card text:** Was there ever a light that didn't shine? Then the Son was always there.  
**Cost 3 · Power 5** · Correct answer to: Once He Was Not

**Explanation:** Athanasius shows how silly 'once He was not' really is. It's like saying a light once had no shine, or a spring once had no water. God is always Father, so He always has His Son.

> "the Light was once without radiance, and the Fountain was once barren and dry"
> — Against the Arians I.14 (NPNF trans.)

**Citation:** St. Athanasius, Against the Arians I.14

### [ ] Spoken as Man  `arg-as-man`

**Card text:** Some words Jesus speaks as God. Some He speaks as man.  
**Cost 2 · Power 4** · Correct answer to: Not Even the Son Knows

**Explanation:** The Son truly became man. He got hungry, He wept, and He spoke of not knowing the hour. Those belong to the human nature He took on for us, not to the Word who made all things.

> "He made this as those other declarations as man by reason of the flesh"
> — Against the Arians III.43 (NPNF trans.)

**Citation:** St. Athanasius, Against the Arians III.43

### [ ] Worship Belongs to God  `arg-worship`

**Card text:** Christians worship the Son. Worshipping a creature would be idolatry.  
**Cost 3 · Power 4** · Not a correct answer to any challenge (partial credit only)

**Explanation:** When Cornelius bowed to Peter, Peter stopped him: 'I myself also am a man.' Angels refuse worship too. But the Son is worshipped, so He is not a creature. He is God.

> "creature does not worship creature, but servant Lord, and creature God"
> — Against the Arians II.23 (NPNF trans.)

**Citation:** St. Athanasius, Against the Arians II.23
